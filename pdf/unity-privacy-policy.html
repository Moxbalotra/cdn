Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how Unity Jewellers collects, uses and protects your information.

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to Unity Jewellers. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use the Unity Jewellers mobile application, website or related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us when you use our services, create an account, browse products, add products to your wishlist or place an order.

Personal Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping and delivery address
  • Billing information, where applicable
  • Account information

Device and Usage Information

We may automatically receive certain technical information when you use the application, such as device type, operating system, application version, IP address and general usage information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Unity Jewellers may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process and manage orders
  • Provide product and shopping services
  • Manage your wishlist and preferences
  • Communicate with you regarding orders and services
  • Improve our products, application and user experience
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Provide customer support
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

3. Account Information

If you create an account with Unity Jewellers, we may use the information associated with your account to provide personalized features and maintain your account.

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and should notify us if you believe your account has been used without authorization.

4. Orders and Payments

When you place an order, certain information may be required to process and deliver your purchase, including your name, contact information and delivery address.

We do not intend to store complete payment card details such as your full card number or CVV on our own servers. Payment information may be processed by authorized third-party payment service providers according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

5. Wishlist and Product Preferences

Unity Jewellers may store information about products that you add to your wishlist or otherwise interact with. This helps us provide wishlist functionality and improve your shopping experience.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or related services may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain functionality, understand usage and improve the user experience.

You may be able to control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some features of the service.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party service providers to support functions such as hosting, analytics, authentication, payment processing, customer support and delivery.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and may have their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Children's Privacy

Unity Jewellers is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental or guardian involvement.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the email address provided below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us.
