Child Safety & CSAE Policy

Shree Vasudev Janseva Trust is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment across its application and services.

Our commitment: Shree Vasudev Janseva Trust strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, exploitation, and any conduct that endangers children.

1. Purpose of This Policy

This Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy explains the standards and procedures followed by Shree Vasudev Janseva Trust to help protect children from sexual abuse and exploitation on or through our application and related services.

This page is publicly accessible and communicates our child-safety standards.

2. Zero-Tolerance Standard

We maintain a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation. Users must not use our application or services to create, upload, request, distribute, promote, facilitate, or otherwise engage in content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

Strictly prohibited:
  • Any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM).
  • Sexual grooming or attempts to build inappropriate relationships with children for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual exploitation, trafficking, or coercion involving children.
  • Sexualized communication or solicitation involving children.
  • Creating, uploading, sharing, requesting, or distributing material that sexually exploits children.
  • Using the service to facilitate or encourage conduct that could place a child at risk of sexual abuse or exploitation.

3. Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

CSAM is illegal and strictly prohibited on our platform. We do not permit the creation, storage, upload, sharing, distribution, or promotion of CSAM through our application or services.

When we obtain actual knowledge of prohibited material or activity, we will take appropriate action consistent with our published standards, applicable laws, and relevant platform requirements. Actions may include removal or restriction of content, suspension or termination of accounts, preservation of relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting to competent authorities or relevant child-safety organizations where required.

4. User Reporting & In-App Safety Mechanism

Users should report suspected child-safety violations as soon as possible. Our application provides or is intended to provide a mechanism for users to submit concerns, feedback, or reports.

Reports should include enough information to help identify the relevant account, content, or activity. Users should not redistribute or forward suspected illegal material while making a report.

5. Review and Enforcement

We review reports and other available information relating to potential CSAE or CSAM violations and take appropriate action consistent with our policies and applicable law.

  • Reported content or accounts may be reviewed.
  • Violating content may be removed or restricted.
  • Accounts may be suspended or permanently terminated.
  • Repeated or serious violations may result in stronger enforcement.
  • Where legally required or appropriate, matters may be reported to relevant authorities or child-safety organizations.

6. Child Safety Laws and Cooperation

Shree Vasudev Janseva Trust aims to comply with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. We cooperate with lawful requests from competent authorities and take appropriate steps when we become aware of conduct that may involve child sexual abuse or exploitation.
